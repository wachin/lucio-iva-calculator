Lucio IVA Calculator

Lucio IVA Calculator calculates VAT excluded, VAT amount, and VAT included values. The three displays are synchronized: when you type in one of them, the other two update automatically using the active rate.

Basic Use

  1. Choose the country or rate from the center header, the percentage button, or the menu.
  2. Click VAT excluded, VAT, or VAT included to choose the value you want to edit.
  3. Enter the amount with the on-screen keypad or with your physical keyboard.
  4. Check the other two values calculated automatically.

Rates and Format

Settings lets you change number separators, decimal places, visual theme, interface size, and language. Custom rates lets you create your own percentages for discounts, fees, or other taxes.
